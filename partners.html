HotelMozil — Affiliate & Partner Information

An AI-powered hotel price-comparison app for Hebrew-speaking Israeli travelers, with a supporting website. This page is a plain-English overview for advertisers, affiliate networks, and reviewers.

What HotelMozil is

HotelMozil is a free mobile app (iOS & Android) plus a companion website, built for Israeli travelers who already know which hotel they want to book. A user pastes the link to a specific hotel page from Booking.com or Agoda into the app. HotelMozil opens a connection and opens that exact booking — same property, same dates, same guests — in the official partner app. More often than not the price shown is lower than the one the user saw. HotelMozil does not compare prices and does not rank offers. The user then completes the booking directly on the official partner site (Booking.com or Agoda), with their own payment method.

How a referral happens, step by step

  1. The user finds a hotel they want on Booking.com or Agoda and pastes that hotel's link into the HotelMozil app.
  2. The app identifies the exact property, dates, and guest count, and compares the rate across markets.
  3. The user taps through to the partner's own hotel page via our affiliate/tracking link, and completes a normal reservation on the partner's site, paying the partner directly.

What we are not

  • We do not sell, list, or promise voucher/coupon codes for any advertiser.
  • We do not process payments, hold card details, or resell inventory.
  • We do not use email spam, adware, toolbars, cookie-stuffing, or brand-term bidding.
  • We do not guarantee savings on every search — some searches show no price difference, and we say so plainly in the app.
