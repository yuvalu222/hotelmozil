# 07 — The words

## Caption length, established earlier `[CORPUS]`

| caption | n | median saves/like |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| hashtags only, no prose | 149 | **0.32** |
| 1-5 words | 78 | 0.25 |
| 6-11 words | 136 | 0.49 |
| 12-24 words | 81 | 0.53 |
| 25-59 words | 49 | 0.49 |
| 60+ words | 31 | **0.61** |

Writing a caption roughly doubles saves per like against posting tags alone.
Hashtag COUNT, by contrast, showed nothing (4-5 tags 0.42, 9-12 tags 0.49,
13+ tags 0.44, against a 0.44 median). The words matter; the tags do not.

## Which words `[CORPUS]`

359 captions with three or more words, top third against bottom third.

**The single clearest instruction in the log:**

| | top third | bottom third |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| caption contains "save" | **31%** | **5%** |
| contains an exclamation mark | 36% | 17% |
| contains a question mark | 14% | 10% |

Asking for the save is six times more common in the decks that get saved. It
is the cheapest change available to us and his captions mostly do not do it.

**Vocabulary over-represented in the top third** (minimum eight uses):

| word | top | bottom | ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| places | 21 | 0 | 22x |
| itinerary | 14 | 0 | 15x |
| free | 10 | 0 | 11x |
| hours | 8 | 0 | 9x |
| spots | 20 | 2 | 7x |
| best | 18 | 2 | 6.3x |
| guide | 15 | 2 | 5.3x |
| save | 38 | 7 | 4.9x |
| miss | 8 | 1 | 4.5x |
| days | 8 | 1 | 4.5x |

This is the vocabulary of **concrete planning**: named places, an itinerary, a
number of hours, a number of days, what is free, what not to miss. It is the
same finding as the triangulated itinerary result in `09-travel.md`, arrived at
from a completely different direction — the words rather than the pictures.

What is absent from the top list is as telling: no adjectives of beauty, no
"stunning", "breathtaking", "paradise".

## Caveats
- Correlation. Captions that say "save this" are written by people who also
  structure the deck for saving.
- The corpus is overwhelmingly English; whether "שמרו" behaves the same in
  Hebrew is untested. His own best post did use a written Hebrew caption.
- Destination names in the list (Budapest, Amsterdam, Rome, Positano) reflect
  which destinations happened to be harvested, not that naming them helps.
