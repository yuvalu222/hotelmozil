# Hebrew covers — what the first slide says (10.10)

Data: TikTok photo search, 54 Hebrew queries → 1,117 Hebrew carousels from
756 creators (`harvest/he-search/`), every cover transcribed verbatim
(`coded/*.jsonl`, blind codes). 613 are travel covers from accounts with ≥200
followers. Reach measure: plays / followers (search cross-section).
Cross-checked against 1,548 English covers measured within-creator
(`cover-rows.json`) and his own 13 travel covers.

⚠️ The within-creator Hebrew test (full grids of 145 Israeli creators) was
refused by TikTok after 6 creators (IP throttle, 10.10 15:15). Pending.

## Hebrew only, strong but small n

* "כמה עלה לנו…" (cost reveal): ×12, 5 of 6 above median. 521,500 plays on
  2,147 followers. His own two cost covers sit mid-top of his 13.
* "טסים ל…?" opening: ×2.97, 67% above median, n=36.
* "אסור": ×1.76, n=33.

## Avoid

* Long cover text (≥15 words): ×0.60 [0.40, 0.90].
* "טופ N": his three "טופ 3" covers are his bottom three (632 – 2,391).
* "…שאסור לכם לפספס… (ככה חסכתי כסף)": his own, bottom three.

## Corrections to earlier claims

* "חלק 2 halves views" is wrong for reach: Hebrew ×2.10 (n=34), English ×1.16
  [0.83, 2.04]. The −56% measured on 6.10 was saves-per-like in captions.
  "חלק 2" still adds nothing, so it is not used.
* Question mark: neutral in Hebrew (×1.02, n=123); English −10% (Upworthy).
