# 14 — The Israeli audience

## Volume
`[DATA]` Israeli outbound travel reached ~**9.42 million trips in 2025**, past
the pre-pandemic peak. **3.3 million trips in Jan-June 2026.** August 2026 set
a record at **1.4 million departures in the month**.

This is a large, active market, and it is growing — the audience exists.

## Where they actually go
`[DATA]` Top five outbound flight destinations from Tel Aviv, 2025:

1. **Greece**
2. United States
3. **United Arab Emirates**
4. **Cyprus**
5. **Italy**

`[DATA]` **Egypt is the most visited destination overall** for Israeli
travellers. **Cyprus is the most visited in Europe.**

**The clearest gap is Egypt** — the single most visited destination, with
nothing made for it. Sinai in particular is a short, cheap, high-volume Israeli
trip. Cyprus is also under-served at one deck for the most-visited European
destination.

**And a caution about Thailand:** his existing feed is Thailand-dominant, and
Thailand is not in the top five. It may still be right — it is the big
backpacking trip and draws a specific young audience — but it is not where the
volume is.

## Trends named for 2026
`[LIT]` Short- and medium-haul preference; "soft travel" aimed at stress
relief; family sun-and-beach; short "revenge travel" escapes; a stated
preference for destinations perceived as welcoming.

That matches our `tt-19` (everything under four hours) better than anything
else in the library.
