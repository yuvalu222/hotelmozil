# 15 — How often to post

`[LIT]` The clearest actionable numbers found tonight, and they apply directly
to an account of this size.

| change | effect |
| --- | --- |
| daily vs 2-3x per week | **3.5x faster follower growth** |
| 1-4 posts per day | up to 56% higher engagement than sporadic posting |
| 1x weekly -> 2-5x weekly | +17% views per post |
| -> 6-10x weekly | +29% views per post |
| 3+ per week | 2.5x higher monthly follower growth (>8%) |

`[DATA]` Accounts under 2,000 followers average just over **2 posts a week** —
so the bar to beat the field is low.

`[LIT]` Small accounts under 5,000 followers that held a regular schedule saw
269% annual follower growth.

## What this means here, plainly
The library already holds 25 finished decks. At one a day that is close to a
month of posting without producing anything new, and the difference between
that and the current sporadic pace is, by these numbers, the single largest
lever available — larger than any individual deck improvement.

**Caveat:** every figure above is practitioner/aggregator data, not a
controlled study, and the causality is not clean — accounts that post daily are
also accounts doing other things right. Treat the direction as solid and the
multipliers as optimistic.
