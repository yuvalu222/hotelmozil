# When and how often to post (16.10)

## His own data (the deciding source)
- TikTok, 37 posts since 13.8 (Studio item_list, his-posts-16-10.json). Median views by posting hour, Israel time:
  morning 9-13 ~5K (12 posts), afternoon 15-17 ~8K (7), evening 18:00-21:30 ~13K (12).
  4 of his top 5 posts went up in the evening (49K, 40K, 37K, 25K).
- Friday weak (2.4K, 942, 2.0K). Saturday night strong (49K and 11K at 21:40). Thursday weak (14K, 5.8K, 1.7K, 639, 188).
- Follower activity by hour (Studio, follower-active-hours-16-10.json, Asia/Jerusalem): plateau 14:00-23:00, peak 19:00-22:00. Days nearly flat (1179-1194 active per day).
- 95% of views come from For You, so content and photos outweigh time slot.
- His best stretch (15.8-6.9) was near-daily posting; after an 18-day gap views dropped.
- Instagram @hotelmozil_app: 0 posts, 4 followers. No own data yet.

## Large studies
- Buffer, 11.4M TikTok posts / 150K accounts: vs 1/week, 2-5/week = +17% views per post, 6-10 = +29%, 11+ = +34%;
  median views per post stay flat, "the more you post, the greater your chances of going viral with a single post".
  https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
- Buffer, 2.1M Instagram posts / 102K accounts: vs 1-2/week, 3-5 = +12% reach per post, 6-9 = +18%, 10+ = +24%;
  follower growth 0.12% / 0.26% / 0.44% / 0.66%. Reels, photos and carousels were not segmented.
  https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/
- Best global hours disagree between Sprout (Tue-Thu 14-18) and Buffer (Sun 9, Mon 13, Sat 15-17), so his own data decides.

## Decision
- 5 posts a week on TikTok, one a day, never two: Saturday night 20:30, Sunday-Wednesday 19:00. No Thursday, no Friday.
- Instagram gets the same post at the same time (4:5 set, at most 10 slides).

## More than one a day (16.10, owner: "on autopilot there is no reason not to post more than one a day if it helps")
- His own same-day pairs (TikTok): 15.8 10:11 29K + 21:43 49K; 22.8 10:48 8.9K + 17:39 22.6K; 2.9 09:07 1.4K + 12:30 24.7K;
  9.9 12:58 26 + 16:52 8.9K. The second post of the day did not collapse; on 3 of 4 days it beat the first.
- Buffer's top bucket (11+ a week, which means more than one a day) had the highest views per post (+34% vs 1/week).
  Median stays flat, so extra posts buy more chances at a breakout, not a better typical post.
- "Cannibalization" claims for 2-3 a day come from blogs with no data; the only number given is 4-5+ a day as the risk zone.
- Instagram: Mosseri in an AMA (reported by thesocialtea, July 2026, no primary link) said a carousel and a reel the same day
  won't hurt reach; Buffer's 10+/week bucket had +24% reach per post and the fastest follower growth.
- Limit is production, not the platforms: every deck must pass the WOW photo bar.

## Proposal
- 11 a week: Sat night 20:30 + 22:00, Sun-Wed 19:00 + 21:30, Thu 19:00, Fri none. Same on Instagram.
- Ramp: start at one a day while the stock of ready decks covers it, go to two a day once production keeps up, and check views per post in Studio each week.
