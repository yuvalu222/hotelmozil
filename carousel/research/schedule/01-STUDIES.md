# Upload time and frequency: large studies only (16.10)

Owner: his ~37 posts are too few for a statistical answer; decide from studies of millions of posts.
Two research agents read the primary pages; the three rows marked (checked) were re-read by me.

## TikTok
- Buffer 2025, 1M posts, MEDIAN VIEWS BY UPLOAD TIME, local time (checked, archive 6.7.2025):
  "best time to post on TikTok is Sunday at 8 p.m., followed by Tuesday at 4 p.m., and Wednesday at 5 p.m."
  "views tend to pick up later in the day, with peak time slots at 1 p.m. or later."
- Metricool 2026, 2,314,756 posts / 92,000+ accounts, views (checked):
  "The hours between 6 PM and 8 PM contain the heaviest concentrations of views throughout the entire week."
  Best per day: 8 PM (Sat 7 PM). Dead zone 2-4 AM.
- Metricool 2023, 1,907,846 videos: "best time to post on TikTok is at 8 pm every day of the week, except on Saturdays ... 6 pm".
- Sprout (2B engagements) and Buffer 2026 (7.1M) measure engagement, not views by upload time; their days contradict each other.
  Day-of-week tables assume a Sat-Sun weekend, so they do not carry over to Israel. Hour of day does.
- Frequency, Buffer 11.4M posts / 150K accounts, fixed effects (each account vs itself) (checked):
  median views 489 / 506 / 487 / 459 and p90 3,722 / 6,983 / 10,092 / 14,401 for 1 / 2-5 / 6-10 / 11+ a week.
  Typical post flat; the chance of a breakout rises ~4x. "relative gains hold true across all account sizes".
- TikTok for Business (official, 2021, no data): "Aim to post 1-4 times per day to test how different types of content are received."
- No primary data for: cannibalization at 2-3 a day, minimum spacing, "post 30-60 min before peak", share of views in the first hour.
- Metricool 2026: 96% of reach within 10 days. Socialinsider (6M posts): surge in days 1-5.
- Carousel vs video reach: conflicting (Metricool video 5x views; Fanpage Karma carousel reach +3%).

## Instagram
- No study measures carousel reach by upload hour, none covers Israel; engagement peaks across studies are up to 14 h apart.
- Frequency, Buffer 2.1M posts, fixed effects: reach per post +12% / +18% / +24% at 3-5 / 6-9 / 10+ a week vs 1-2; follower growth
  0.12 / 0.26 / 0.44 / 0.66%. Mosseri (Reel, Dec 2024): "The more you post, the more reach you'll achieve."
- Small accounts: Fanpage Karma 2026 (3M+ posts): under 10K followers, "Reels reach nearly three times as many non-followers as carousels".
- Mosseri (Oct 2024): carousels get a "second chance" (slide 2 shown to people who did not swipe);
  "I highly advise you to add music so it's eligible to show up in video experiences, like the Reels tab."
- Instagram originality rules: visible watermarks make content ineligible for recommendations; the mirror must upload clean images.

## Decision (proposal to the owner)
- TikTok: 2 posts a day at 17:00 and 20:00 Israel time, every day. Inside the window where the views-by-upload-time studies agree.
- Instagram: the same two, clean images, with music; plus a Reel version of each deck to reach non-followers.
- Review after 2 weeks (Metricool 2023: frequency effects show "after 2 weeks of maintaining a high posting frequency").
