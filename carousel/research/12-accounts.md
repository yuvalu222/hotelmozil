## What is actually consistent here

**Slide count. Every one of the fourteen sits between 6 and 11.** No account
with a repeatable system runs long decks. This is the strongest single piece of
evidence in the whole log for the band, because it is not a population average
— it is fourteen independent creators who each landed on the same range.

## What is NOT consistent, and I am not going to pretend otherwise

**Caption length.** The best account writes 118 words; the second best writes
none. Six of the fourteen write zero. So the corpus-wide caption finding
(tags-only 0.32 vs 12-24 words 0.53 vs 60+ words 0.61) holds as a tendency but
is clearly not a requirement, and something else is carrying @japanonabudget.

**Sample caveat, stated plainly:** three decks per account is a tiny sample and
a median of three numbers is fragile. Treat the ranking as a shortlist of
accounts worth studying, not as a league table.

## The one worth studying hardest
@vitortrip: six slides, 1.39 saves per like, 97,000 median saves. That is more
saves than likes, sustained across three posts. Their structure is the dense
itinerary stack already described in TIKTOK.md section 9c.
