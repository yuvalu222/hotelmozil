**Correlation between saves/like and shares/like: +0.62.** Of the top 120 decks
by saves and the top 120 by shares, **61 appear in both**.

The caption vocabulary of save-heavy and share-heavy decks is close to
identical — both lists lead with the same planning words.

## Conclusion
There is **no distinct "shareable" content strategy** to chase alongside the
save strategy. They are largely the same content. And since saves run about
3.7x more common than shares, saves remain the right thing to optimise.

Recorded because a null result stops effort being split, which is worth as much
as a positive finding.
