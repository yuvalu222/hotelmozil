# Viral carousel research — the learning log

Owner's instruction, 5.10: treat understanding viral content as training a new
model. Collect data from every direction until the data knows better than any
of us. He gives direction; reality decides.

Rules this log runs on:
- **Assume I do not know.** Every claim here carries its source, and claims
  from my own corpus carry the sample and the caveat.
- **Separate measured from read.** `[CORPUS]` = measured on the 524 harvested
  decks. `[LIT]` = outside research. `[PRACTICE]` = practitioner consensus,
  weakest evidence.
- **Record what overturned what.** A finding that corrects an earlier one is
  more valuable than a new one, and the correction is written down, not
  quietly swapped.
- Nothing here edits a slide. Decisions come later, from the accumulated body.
