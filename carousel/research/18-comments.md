# 18 — Comments, and whether to chase them

## The number
`[LIT]` Posts with a **question in the caption earn 26.19% more comments**,
measured across 2.31 million posts (Metricool). That is one of the few hard,
large-sample figures in this whole log.

## But weight them correctly for this format
`[LIT]` Comments are a genuine ranking signal — the algorithm reads how fast
and how deeply a post triggers conversation. **However, comments are weighted
less for carousels than for video**, and a carousel with many saves and few
comments still performs.

`[LIT]` Comments per view run higher on carousels than video even so. Benchmark
**above 0.5% comments per view** as healthy. A question on the FIRST SLIDE (not
just the caption) spikes the rate.

## So the order of effort is
1. **saves** — the primary lever for this format
2. **completion** — see `01-algorithm.md`
3. comments — real, cheap to get, but not worth distorting a deck for

## Checked against our own captions
Most already close on a question: *באיזו אתם קופצים ראשונה?* · *מה הייתם
מוסיפים?* · *איזו עשיתם?* · *באיזה הייתם?*

That was done by copying the source decks' shape, not from knowing why. It is
right, and it should stay.

**The cheap upgrade not yet made:** the question currently lives only in the
caption. Putting it **on the last content slide as well** is what the sources
say spikes the rate, and costs nothing.

## Series and parts
`[PRACTICE]` Found only weak material. The storytime shape invites a "what
happened next" reply without needing a call to action, and works as a
multi-part series. **No solid evidence found** that part 2 inherits reach from
part 1, or that numbering parts helps distribution. Our `tt-10` and `tt-12` are
built as part 1 of a series on that assumption, which is currently unsupported
either way.
