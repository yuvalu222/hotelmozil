## Batch-level observations

Stated only for the 12 decks I saw.

**1. Every single ad in this batch (8/8, A1) has ZERO text on the creative. Every single organic
post (4/4, A2) has text on slide 1.**
This is the sharpest split in my batch and it is total — no exceptions either way.
- 8 advertisers with no on-slide text: הילה ספא אשדוד (ad-1275122744343773), Home Market - הומרקט
  (ad-166982804643240), Lear Sense - Experience Hotel (ad-5520911621467526 AND ad-1040881642263897
  — same creative, 2 library IDs), אוהד שגב (ad-255958373794072), הביטחוניסטים (ad-655568950212034),
  The Savoy - Tel Aviv (ad-1397118039289931), עיצוב ותכנון פנים - Shir Hemo Kikos
  (ad-1044549735239446), KAPAO THAI FOOD (ad-1426444389346303). That is 7 distinct advertisers
  across 8 ad IDs.
- 4 accounts with a text hook on slide 1: withsumegha, nypost, uaz_findyourenviro, ivskitchen.
- Consequence for a numbered text-on-image listicle maker: the Israeli-market ad set in my batch
  offers essentially no reference material on hook typography. Whatever typographic convention
  exists, it is coming from the organic side, not the paid side.

**2. The strongest image is NOT on slide 1 in a meaningful minority of the photo-only ads — and in
one case the deck opens on an uncropped, letterboxed frame.**
Seen in 3 distinct advertisers: Lear Sense (both IDs: pillarboxed drone shot leads, postcard
rooftop-pool shot sits at the drop-off position), Home Market (letterboxed exterior render leads
with grey bars top and bottom), הביטחוניסטים (the scenic skyline-view frame is slide 2, the
backs-of-heads frame is slide 1). Aspect-ratio mismatch producing grey bars appeared in 4 of 8 ads
— Lear Sense ×2, Home Market, אוהד שגב (inconsistent ratios across its 19 slides). The counter-
example is The Savoy, the only ad that deliberately withholds (detail → room reveal).

**3. Among the organic listicles, the number is ALWAYS in the slide-1 hook, but the per-item
counter on slide 2 is inconsistent — and item titles fall into two distinct schools.**
4 of 4 organic posts put a count or a count-shaped frame in the cover text: withsumegha ("Day
28/60" plus an implied checklist), nypost (implied by "this day", answered as a 5-stat set),
uaz_findyourenviro ("11"), ivskitchen ("10 Things To Do").
- Carry a visible "1." onto slide 2: withsumegha, uaz_findyourenviro (2 accounts).
- Drop the counter and title slide 2 by its proper noun instead: ivskitchen ("📍 Pike Place
  Market"), nypost (labelled stat chips) (2 accounts).
- Title→body schools: label-answer (nypost: question-shaped label + one giant word) and
  proper-noun-plus-diary-note (ivskitchen: place name + first-person practical aside). Two of the
  four carry no body copy on slide 2 at all (uaz_findyourenviro has only the title line;
  withsumegha's slide 2 is all body and no photo).

**4. On-slide emoji are close to absent even in the organic set; emoji live in the caption.**
Only 1 of 4 organic accounts put an emoji on a slide (ivskitchen, a single 📍 as a title bookend).
0 of 8 ads. Meanwhile emoji are dense in the captions of withsumegha (🙌 🩵 😂 ✅), uaz_findyourenviro
(🌿👀), ivskitchen (📍🦪🦀), הילה ספא (🌿💦✨📍), Lear Sense ×2 (📍), KAPAO (🇹🇭🥢), Shir Hemo Kikos (🌿🤍).
So in my batch the emoji convention is a caption convention, not a creative convention.

**5. Highlight boxes and scrims, not outlines or drop shadows, are how the organic hooks get
contrast — except where the creator relied on open sky.**
withsumegha: stacked white and hot-pink highlight boxes, one per line. nypost: dark bottom-up
gradient scrim under the headline. ivskitchen: a soft translucent light box behind the serif line
plus an offset shadow on the script line. uaz_findyourenviro is the outlier — bare white type with
no box, no outline, no shadow, legible on slide 1 only because it sits on blue sky, and already
marginal on slide 2 where it crosses concrete and grass. That is 3 of 4 accounts using a backing
device, and the one that skipped it is the one with the legibility problem.
