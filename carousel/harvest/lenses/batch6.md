## Batch-level observations

Everything below is counted within this batch only (12 decks: 4 organic IG accounts, 8 Meta advertisers).

1. **Total split between the two piles on whether type appears on the image at all.** All 4 organic IG decks carry on-image typography, and it is the primary design element in 3 of them. **All 8 ads carry literally zero on-image text** — no headline, no price, no logo, no watermark, no end-card. Advertisers, all 8 of 8: עומר רצון, Secret Tours, רוני שגב, Share Spa, יוני פארג', Lear Sense, inbal_yosef_interiordesign, Home Market. In every ad the entire proposition (₪5,480 package, reserve-fund reimbursement, phone number, project positioning) lives only in the caption, which is truncated in-feed. This is a genre boundary, not a stylistic one: the ads in this batch are photo-supply, the organic decks are designed artefacts.

2. **Not one ad in the batch has a CTA slide or an end-card.** 8 of 8 advertisers (same list as above) end on an ordinary photo. The only deck with any internal sequencing logic beyond "widest first" is יוני פארג', which alternates process/result and closes on a result; the rest are shot-lists (Lear Sense and Share Spa both do establishing-wide → payoff-detail; רוני שגב, inbal_yosef and Home Market are room-tour order).

3. **Emoji were systematically pushed out of the artwork and into the caption.** In 3 of the 4 organic accounts — nyctheloop (caption 🗽💸🏮🎉, artwork none), la_freebies (caption 📚🧁🍕🍪, artwork none), anushkarathod98 (artwork uses flag glyphs as evidence, not as punctuation) — and in 5 of 8 advertisers whose captions are emoji-dense while the creative is bare (Secret Tours 😲🌴👈👉, Share Spa 🫡, יוני פארג' ✔️×6 as bullets, Lear Sense 📍, inbal_yosef 📣📍✨). The single emoji rendered *on* artwork anywhere in this batch is the 🇨🇭 closing the switzerlandersss hook.

4. **The serif is the organic house style, and it is used in a specific way: serif for the title, grotesque for the body.** All 4 organic accounts use a high-contrast display serif for at least the hook or the item title — anushkarathod98 ("7 TRAVEL HACKS" and every item title), nyctheloop (whole body of slide 2), la_freebies (cover and item body), switzerlandersss (cover and place name). Three of the four (anushkarathod98, nyctheloop, la_freebies) pair it against a heavy grotesque in the same deck, switching family by role rather than by slide.
