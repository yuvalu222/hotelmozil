Crucially the boxes are **per-line and width-hugging** — each line's box shrinks to that line's own text, so a 3-line caption produces three stacked white blocks of different widths with a ragged silhouette. This is the native Instagram Stories "background/highlight" text style, pasted straight into a feed carousel. It is the single most reliable legibility device in the batch: it survives any background, including the busy market stall on slide 10 and the blown sky on slide 1.

## Batch-level observations

**Advertiser count: 12 decks, 12 distinct advertisers** (no two decks in this batch share an advertiser). Several decks are themselves duplicated *outside* the batch — see the duplicates note below.

### Where the wave-1 claims held, and where they broke

1. **"Almost every travel ad carries ZERO text on the creative; all copy lives in the caption."** — **Held for 9 of 12 decks** (Shir Hemo Kikos, fastenupyourseatbelts, sisi.on.the.go, Africa Adventure Vacations, Natura Oasis, Wira Krisna, Falkensteiner, Discover Puerto Rico, GolfNow). **Broken by 3 advertisers: madetoroamfam** (text on 10 of 11 slides), **AYANA Phuket** (text on 4 of 4), and **partially Chrononight** (text on 1 of 2). So the text-on-image rate here is 2 fully-committed decks out of 12 — ~17%, which is higher than wave 1 suggested but still a clear minority.
2. **"Almost none ends on a CTA/logo/price slide."** — **Held for 11 of 12.** **Broken by exactly one advertiser: AYANA Phuket**, whose slide 4 carries the price (`15MB*`), a `SIGN UP` button, a `GET EXCLUSIVE DEALS` sub-line and an award badge. Every other deck — including a €9 product (Natura Oasis), an 8-offer marketplace (GolfNow) and a 64-day DMO campaign (Discover Puerto Rico) — simply stops on a photo.
3. **"Emoji appear in captions, not on artwork."** — **Broken by 1 advertiser: madetoroamfam**, slide 6, a 😀 typed inline inside the white sticker bar. Held for the other 11, where emoji are dense in captions (🌿🤍 ✈️💑💕🧳🎯 ✨🌍✈ 🤫🌊 🌞🌴 ⛳️) and absent from every pixel of artwork. Note the pattern: the one deck with on-slide emoji is the one built in the Instagram Stories editor, where emoji are one tap away — the tool predicts the exception.
4. **"Aspect-ratio letterboxing (grey bars) is common on the ad side."** — **Strongly held: 10 of 12 decks show grey bars on at least one slide.** Only **AYANA Phuket** (square, full-bleed, all four) and **Discover Puerto Rico** (wide-in-wide) are clean. Worse, letterboxing is often **inconsistent within a single deck** — Shir Hemo Kikos mixes top/bottom bars and left/right bars in one five-slide deck; GolfNow's bar thickness changes slide to slide so the image height jumps as you swipe; Wira Krisna letterboxes 1 of 5. Falkensteiner and Natura Oasis are the extreme cases, with bars on 7/7 and 4/4 slides respectively.

### Patterns across advertisers

- **The caption carries structure the artwork refuses to carry — 4 advertisers** (Natura Oasis: "131-page, 9€" never on a slide; GolfNow: four named offers incl. `£30 or Less`, each with its own card headline, none on artwork; Africa Adventure Vacations: a day-by-day itinerary, artwork ordered by animal; Chrononight: a numbered 1-2-3 reset list, artwork has none of it). This is the batch's dominant failure and the clearest opening for a text-on-image listicle: **four advertisers already wrote the listicle, then declined to draw it.**
- **No unifying colour grade across slides — 7 advertisers** (fastenupyourseatbelts, Africa Adventure Vacations, Natura Oasis (half-graded), Wira Krisna, GolfNow, Chrononight, madetoroamfam). Only 4 decks read as one visual set: AYANA (designed system), Discover Puerto Rico (campaign grade), Falkensteiner (brand-shoot grade), Shir Hemo Kikos (single-shoot grade). madetoroamfam is the interesting case — **no grade at all, yet the deck still coheres**, because the white-sticker device does the work a grade would normally do. For a Hebrew listicle-maker that is the load-bearing lesson: a consistent type device substitutes for a consistent photo library.
- **Slide 2 is used as filler — 10 of 12 advertisers.** In ten decks slide 2 is simply "another photo" with no hook, no numbering, no continuation.
