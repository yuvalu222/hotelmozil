## Batch-level observations

Counted within this batch only (13 decks; 4 organic Instagram accounts, 7 distinct
advertisers across 9 ads).

1. **Not one Meta ad in this batch put any text on any slide. 0 of 9 ads, 7 of 7
   advertisers.** CABANA - Wooden Dreams, Home Market (הומרקט), Sweet Holiday By Adika,
   יוני פארג׳ אילת עיצוב שיער (3 separate ads), Lear Sense - Experience Hotel,
   רשת שר ספא / Share Spa Chain, UniqTravel by Esther — every one is pure photography with
   no headline, no price, no date, no logo and no CTA card baked into the creative. The
   whole offer lives in the Hebrew caption. In the most extreme cases (Sweet Holiday,
   UniqTravel, Share Spa) the caption contains exact dates, flight times, a named hotel and
   a phone number, and the image carries none of it.

2. **On-image text is an organic-only behaviour here. 3 of 4 organic accounts, 0 of 7
   advertisers.** moresocialclub, enjoythewood_ and somewhere.in.arkansas all burn a hook
   into slide 1; nasha_bhardwaj does not (it is a photo dump). Each of the three uses a
   different contrast device rather than bare text on photo: a flat cream colour field with
   black serif (moresocialclub), a torn white paper card as a local scrim plus a white pill
   box (enjoythewood_), and empty sky as the scrim plus a yellow highlight box
   (somewhere.in.arkansas). Nobody in this batch set text directly on busy pixels.

3. **Emojis are a caption device, not a slide device — 12 of 13 decks have zero emoji on
   any slide.** Across all 11 accounts/advertisers the only on-slide instance is the single
   red pin on somewhere.in.arkansas slide 1, and it decorates rather than replaces a word.
   Meanwhile the Israeli ad captions are emoji-dense (Sweet Holiday, UniqTravel, Share Spa,
   Lear Sense, יוני פארג׳), which makes the contrast explicit: the same advertiser who
   writes plane/hotel/star emojis in the caption puts nothing on the image.

4. **Announcing a count is common; printing the number is not.** 2 of 11 accounts announce
   a count in the hook (moresocialclub "6", somewhere.in.arkansas "10 THINGS TO DO IN"), but
   only moresocialclub prints a per-item numeral — and it prints "00", a decorative editorial
   numeral rather than a working ordinal. somewhere.in.arkansas keeps its keycap numbers
   entirely in the caption. enjoythewood_ enumerates nothing and titles items as imperative
   sentences with full stops.

5. **Slide 2 is usually a variant of slide 1, not a second reason. 6 of 9 ads, 5
   advertisers.** Sweet Holiday By Adika (same skyline, day then night), CABANA (the same
   wooden rail in a second room), Home Market (interchangeable interiors) and all three
   יוני פארג׳ ads (result shot after result shot) put a near-duplicate at the
   highest-drop-off position. The two exceptions that place a genuinely stronger frame
   second are Lear Sense (rooftop pool with a guest) and UniqTravel (children catching balls
   in the wave pool).

6. **Wide creative letterboxed into a taller slide frame — 4 advertisers.** Home Market,
   Sweet Holiday By Adika, UniqTravel by Esther, and inconsistently Lear Sense (a tall
   portrait slide followed by a landscape one). These decks visibly change size mid-swipe
   and leave empty bands; the assets were made elsewhere and dropped in.

7. **No ad in this batch closes with anything. 9 of 9 ads, 7 advertisers.** Every complete
   A1 deck simply stops on another photograph — no CTA card, no price card, no logo frame,
   no "click the link". The only call to action anywhere is the Meta link strip that sits
   outside the creative.

8. **The two decks that look most art-directed are also the two with no people and no text.**
   CABANA (one tight oat/oak palette across 3 slides, 195 run days — the longest runner in
   the batch) and Home Market (uniform CGI grade across 4 renders). Every deck whose images
   were clearly assembled from a supplied library — UniqTravel, Share Spa, יוני פארג׳ —
   shows visible grade and resolution drift between slides. Run-day baselines are null
   throughout, so nothing about performance can be concluded from this.
