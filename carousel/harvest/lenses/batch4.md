## Batch-level observations

Counts below are of **different accounts/advertisers within batch 4 only** (12 decks, 12 distinct accounts — no account repeats in this batch).

1. **Text-on-image exists almost exclusively in the organic Instagram pile; the ads are nearly all text-free creative.** All 4 A2 decks carry copy on the image (travel2losangeles, vietnamessence_tours, la_freebies, veeceecheng). Of the 8 A1 ads, **7 have zero text on every slide** (noyperetz_studio has text on slide 1 only, and it is a logo, not copy): בסט סטונס, התאחדות המלונות בישראל, Master Brow Poli, יוני פארג', R.N. Pastries, Home Market, Superluxurystays. Exactly **one** advertiser — רמי גרינברג / עיריית פתח תקווה — builds a typographic, templated, slide-by-slide deck. So in this batch the numbered-text-on-image genre the study is about is an organic-side form, and the ad side outsources 100% of its language to the caption.

2. **"20 things" decks that number nothing.** Both listicles that promise a count in the hook — travel2losangeles ("20 Things to do") and vietnamessence_tours ("20 Things To Do In") — show **no numerals at all** on the item slides. 2 accounts. The two other organic listicles don't promise a count either: veeceecheng uses no number, la_freebies enumerates by **date** ("SEP 7-13" → "SEP 9"). And the one templated ad deck (רמי גרינברג) enumerates by a **rhyming verb chain** (התפעלנו / התרגשנו / התפתחנו / התרשמנו / התרחבנו) instead of digits. 5 accounts in total use a non-numeric enumerator where a number would be expected.

3. **The white rounded highlight box is the default contrast device in the organic pile — but not universal.** 2 of 4 A2 accounts use it identically (travel2losangeles, vietnamessence_tours): black bold sans inside a white rounded box, no scrim, no shadow.
