## Batch-level observations

**1. On-image text is almost absent outside the organic pile — 8 of 12 decks carry literally zero words in-frame.**
Appeared in **7 different advertisers**: Iren travel (ad-1223423135805880), guymotola (ad-3291941784315436), Hotel Metropolitan TLV (ad-2086293415583169), יוני פארג' אילת עיצוב שיער (both ad-1413719938799857 and ad-190668832155268), מלון ספא וילג׳ (ad-5595289670695615), Stone wall Israel (ad-947387308392865), יעדים טיולי תרבות וטבע (ad-1608330980732051). In every one of these the entire argument — price, destination list, phone number, even the enumeration — lives in the Facebook caption above the carousel, and the slides are a bare photo reel. All four A2 organic decks (lexilaube, thechortshow, vnexpress.guide, thetaiwanderers) do the opposite: text is burned into the image on slide 1 without exception. Within this batch the split between "text-on-image" and "photo-only" maps almost perfectly onto organic-vs-ad, with exactly one crossing case (below).

**2. The only ad advertiser that builds designed text cards, מונה טורס, is also the only one using a hard contrast device — and it uses the strongest one in the batch.**
1 of 8 advertisers (מונה טורס, ad-1431186982197538). Its navy knock-out headline bar plus white sub-card plus orange circular price sticker is the only lockup in the batch where the text cannot be lost against the photo. Compare the contrast methods actually observed across the batch: drop-shadow-only (thechortshow), bottom scrim (vnexpress.guide), white opaque card (thetaiwanderers), flat colour field (thetaiwanderers slide 1), no scrim at all with white type straight on sky (lexilaube). Five decks, five different contrast solutions, and only two of them (מונה טורס, thetaiwanderers) would survive a bright photo.

**3. Slide 2 — the drop-off slot — is spent on a near-duplicate or the weakest asset in 5 of 12 decks, across 5 different advertisers.**
Hotel Metropolitan TLV (same room, second angle), מונה טורס (Hanoi: the most generic headline and a higher price than the card before it, while the $849 bargain waits at slide 3), Stone wall Israel (generic bathroom after a dramatic opener), יעדים (undifferentiated landscape after a striking face), Iren travel (the calmest, least kinetic image in a deck about waterparks). Against that, only 2 decks put real new information at position 2: lexilaube (the entire concept — the Maps screenshot — is the slide-2 payoff) and thetaiwanderers (title + a fact you did not know). This is the clearest repeated structural weakness I saw.

**4. Explicit "keep swiping" signalling barely exists anywhere in the batch, and where it exists it is verbal, not graphic.**
Of 12 decks: lexilaube uses an **unfinished sentence** split across the swipe ("I'm not saying we SHOULD, but…" → "we coulddd.."); thechortshow uses a **count** ("9 essential travel hacks") plus a small blue arrow glyph — the only graphic swipe cue on any slide in the batch; vnexpress.guide uses a count ("15 THINGS TO DO IN"); Stone wall Israel puts the instruction in the **caption** ("6 קירות, 6 סיפורים… החליקו ותראו"), never on the image. The remaining 8 decks give the viewer no reason at all to move to slide 2. 3 different accounts/advertisers use a numeric open loop; exactly 1 account uses an arrow.

**5. Emoji usage in-frame is rare and is concentrated in one organic deck; emoji in the caption is near-universal and tells you nothing about the slides.**
Only **1 of 12 decks** (vnexpress.guide) puts emoji on the image, and it uses them structurally — a four-emoji icon row as a visual contents list, and 📍 substituting for the word "location". The other 11 have zero on-slide emoji, including decks whose captions are dense with them (Iren travel 🏝️🏖️🏄🏽‍♂️🏊🏽‍♀️🎯, thechortshow ✈️⌛👉🏼👇🏻, יוני פארג' ☎️👇👇👇👇⬅️, Hotel Metropolitan 🌊, יעדים 📩). 5 different accounts/advertisers show that caption-emoji → on-slide-emoji does not transfer.

**6. Numbered listicles are rarer than the genre label suggests: only 1 deck in 12 puts a numeral on a card.**
thechortshow alone (ad hoc "1." inline, same size and weight as the body, no badge, no display numeral). vnexpress.guide announces a count on the hook ("15") and then numbers nothing; Stone wall Israel announces "6" in the caption and numbers nothing; מונה טורס enumerates five destinations with zero numerals and carries the rhythm purely through an identical repeated layout; thetaiwanderers has no count anywhere. So across 5 accounts/advertisers that are structurally listicles, **4 enumerate without numbering** — the repeated template, or the count in the hook, does the work the numeral would have done.

**7. Creator-presence vs stock is a clean organic/ad divide here, and it is legible at a glance.**
Person-in-frame shot by the creator: lexilaube (slide 1), thechortshow (both slides), thetaiwanderers (slide 1) — 3 of 4 organic accounts, and in each case the person is the hook image. On the ad side, the people who appear are **models on a commercial shoot** (מלון ספא וילג׳: one couple, one wardrobe, five slides) or **the business owner with clients** (יוני פארג', both ads) — never a first-person creator addressing the lens. The three ad decks with no people at all (Hotel Metropolitan TLV, Stone wall Israel, and Iren travel apart from two rafters in slide 4) are also the three with the least going on per frame.

**8. Two advertisers run the "grid-of-proof opener, then single results" template, and both are the same advertiser — so treat it as 1 pattern, not 2.**
יוני פארג' אילת עיצוב שיער, ads ad-1413719938799857 and ad-190668832155268: both open on an identical-format 3×3 edge-packed photo collage with no gutters and no text, then run three single full-bleed result photos. **9. None of the 8 photo-only ad decks ends on a CTA, price or logo card.**
7 advertisers (all those listed in observation 1). Every one of them simply stops on another photograph — including מונה טורס, which drops its price badge on the final slide and closes on brand copy instead of a number. In this batch, **no deck at all uses its last slide as a close**; the conversion is left entirely to the native ad CTA button below the carousel.

**10. Caption promises content the images do not contain, in at least 2 advertisers.**
Hotel Metropolitan TLV sells "שקיעות" and "רעש של הגלים" over two interior room photographs with no sea, sky or sunset in either frame. guymotola tags #vacation/#חופשה on a fitness post whose only travel signal is a pergola in the background of slide 2.
